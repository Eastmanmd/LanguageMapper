# LanguageMapper

**Live site: https://eastmanmd.github.io/LanguageMapper/**

An interactive atlas of Africa's languages: 2,236 languages mapped where they are spoken, grouped into their families, and compared by their basic words.

- **Africa atlas** — explore every language by family, see which languages are most similar to any one you pick, and zoom into a country for its speakers, families and borders.
- **Map Studio** — make and share your own maps of Africa: language families, density, endangerment, official languages, writing scripts, religion and where any language or family is spoken, over terrain or rivers. Compare two maps side by side, then download as an image or share a link.
- **Nigeria in depth** — a state-by-state map and hand-written profiles for 239 Nigerian languages.
- **Compare languages** — put languages side by side across everyday words and see a similarity tree.

Data: [Glottolog](https://glottolog.org) and the [ASJP Database](https://asjp.clld.org) (CC BY 4.0), [Unicode CLDR](https://cldr.unicode.org), [Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org) via [Our World in Data](https://ourworldindata.org), and [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com).
